Airtime

Bounce, switch shapes in mid-air, and stick the landing upright.

Controls

How to play

Hold Space
Grip and spin while in the air
Release
Open out — the angle freezes, so let go when the halo turns green
Release, then grab again
Change body shape mid-flight: Tuck → Pike → Straddle → Layout. Slower spin, more points, finer control over the landing.
Goal
Chain clean landings for the highest score
Scoring
100–320 per salto depending on shape, +40 for a perfect stick, multiplied by shape variety (up to x2.2) and by the combo
